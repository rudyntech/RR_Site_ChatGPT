Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026
Last updated: September 25, 2026

Who we are

RoadRatings helps riders discover roads and plan rides. It is operated by Rudolfs Namikis, doing business as RoadRatings, based in California, United States.

This policy explains how we handle personal information through RoadRatings-operated websites and the map application that link to this policy. Our services are intended for adults aged 18 and older in the United States.

For privacy questions or requests, contact roadratings@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Google sign-in. When you sign in with Google, we receive your basic account information, including your name, email address, profile picture when available, and an account identifier used to authenticate you. We do not receive your Google password or request access to your Gmail messages, Drive files, or contacts.

Username and saved routes. We collect the username you choose and the routes you save, including route names and selected roads. We associate saved routes with your account so you can retrieve and delete them.

Saved roads can reveal places you are interested in visiting. Saving a route does not establish that you traveled it.

Communications. When you contact us, we receive your email address and any information or attachments you provide.

Technical information and hosting analytics. Our infrastructure providers process technical information to deliver, secure, and monitor the services. This may include IP addresses, browser and device information, request timestamps, and error or security information. We also use basic hosting analytics to understand traffic and service performance.

We do not currently offer device-based precise-location collection, user ratings, photo or video uploads, or payment processing.

How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Sign you in and maintain your account.
  • Associate your username and saved routes with your account.
  • Provide the road discovery and route-saving features you request.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.
  • Monitor reliability, troubleshoot problems, and protect against abuse.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell or rent personal information, provide it to marketing partners for their own marketing, or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Google account information

We use Google account information for sign-in and related account functionality. We do not use Google user data for advertising.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements where applicable.

You can revoke RoadRatings’ connection through your Google Account’s third-party connections settings. This may prevent further Google sign-in but does not automatically delete your RoadRatings account or previously stored information. To request deletion, email roadratings@gmail.com.

Visibility and disclosure

Your username and saved routes are not currently displayed to other users or made publicly available through RoadRatings. Public profiles and route-sharing features are not currently offered.

The operator and service providers may access information as necessary to operate, support, and protect the service.

Our current providers include:

  • Google, for Google sign-in.
  • Supabase, for account authentication and data storage.
  • Cloudflare, for hosting, delivery, security, and basic hosting analytics.

We disclose information to providers as needed for their functions. We may also disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, respond to security incidents, or protect people’s rights and safety.

Cookies, browser storage, and tracking

Sign-in functionality uses cookies or similar browser storage to maintain authenticated sessions. Blocking or clearing this storage may sign you out or prevent account features from working.

We use basic hosting analytics. We do not currently use advertising pixels, session-recording tools, embedded social feeds, or additional app usage analytics.

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. If our practices change, we will provide any legally required opt-out mechanisms and honor applicable opt-out preference signals, including Global Privacy Control where required.

Retention and deletion

We retain your account information and saved routes until you delete the relevant information or request its deletion. We do not routinely delete accounts solely because they are inactive.

You can delete individual saved routes within the application. Account deletion is currently handled by email rather than through a self-service account setting.

We retain support correspondence and technical records only as reasonably necessary for support, security, legal obligations, and resolving disputes.

Where information must be retained for a legal obligation or a legitimate security investigation, we limit its use to that purpose.

Access, correction, and other requests

Email roadratings@gmail.com to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, including your account.

Please identify the email address associated with your account and explain your request. Do not send passwords or authentication codes.

Changing usernames and deleting accounts through self-service settings are planned features. Until those features are available, contact us for assistance.

We may verify your identity before fulfilling a request to protect your account. We aim to respond within 30 days. If additional time is needed, we will explain why and comply with any applicable legal deadlines.

Deleting your account will remove access to its saved routes. Revoking Google access alone does not delete your RoadRatings information.

You may also have rights under applicable privacy laws. We will honor those rights where they apply and will not unlawfully discriminate against you for exercising them.

Security and processing locations

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect personal information. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

RoadRatings is operated from California. Our providers may process information in other locations.

Adults-only service

RoadRatings is intended for adults aged 18 and older in the United States. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

If you believe a minor has provided personal information, contact roadratings@gmail.com so we can investigate and delete it as appropriate.

External links

Our services contain links to independent websites, including Google-hosted resources and social media pages. Their privacy policies govern information they collect when you visit or use them.

Future features and changes

We plan to introduce additional features, including favorite roads, user ratings, photo and video uploads, analytics, precise-location functionality, and payments through an external payment processor.

We will update this policy before introducing materially different data practices and provide any required notices, permissions, or choices. Mentioning a planned feature does not authorize collection for that feature today.

We also plan to establish RoadRatings LLC. Until responsibility is formally transferred, Rudolfs Namikis remains the operator. We will update the operator information and provide any required notice when that changes.

When this policy changes, we will update the date above. We will announce material changes through a prominent notice on the service or another appropriate channel and obtain consent where required.
